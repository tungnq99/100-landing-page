Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our yoga classes

What should I bring to a yoga class?

Bring a yoga mat, comfortable clothing, water bottle, and a towel. We provide all other necessary equipment.

Do I need prior yoga experience to join?

No prior experience needed! We offer beginner-friendly classes and our instructors provide modifications for all levels.

What are the class sizes?

We keep our classes intimate with a maximum of 15 students to ensure personalized attention from instructors.

How do I sign up for a class?

You can book classes through our website, mobile app, or by calling our studio directly.
